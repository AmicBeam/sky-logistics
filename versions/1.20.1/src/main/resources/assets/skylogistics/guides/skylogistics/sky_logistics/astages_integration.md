# AStages Rate Integration

If a modpack uses the AStages integration, gaining stages can gradually increase how much wireless nodes, external interfaces, and simple pipes move at once for the line owner.

Items, fluids, Mekanism chemicals, FE, Botania mana, and Ars Nouveau Source unlock separately. The highest amount from your current stages is used, but devices and targets still keep their own limits.

Stages increase the amount moved at once. They do not make nodes work more often and do not replace Slot Parallel Upgrades. Simple pipes and target devices still keep their own speeds.

Wireless lines normally follow the player who created or claimed them, while simple pipes follow their placer. In multiplayer, lines built by different players may therefore have different unlock progress.
