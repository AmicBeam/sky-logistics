# Vanilla Advancement Rates

If a modpack uses vanilla advancement integration, completing selected Minecraft advancements can gradually increase how much a line moves at once. A Sky Logistics advancement tab shows the unlock path.

Items, fluids, chemicals, FE, mana, and Source can unlock separately. A line uses the highest amount its owner has reached, but devices and targets still keep their own limits.
